Turkion maqolasida qo‘shimcha ma’lumotni tartibli ko‘rsatish uchun matn komponentlaridan foydalanish mumkin. Komponentlar tarixiy da’voga manba o‘rnini bosmaydi: tekshiriladigan faktlar uchun havola ham kerak.

## Ochiladigan bo‘lim

`accordion` va `accordion-item` uzun izohlarni yashirib, kerak bo‘lganda ochishga yordam beradi. Muhim xulosani faqat yopiq bo‘lim ichida qoldirmang.

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0"
---
  :::accordion
    ::::accordion-item{label="Manba qanday ko‘rsatiladi?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
    Muallif, asar nomi va tegishli sahifani yozing. Onlayn manba bo‘lsa, havola qo‘shing.
    ::::
  :::

#code
```mdc
::accordion

:::accordion-item{label="Manba qanday ko‘rsatiladi?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Muallif, asar nomi va tegishli sahifani yozing. Onlayn manba bo‘lsa, havola qo‘shing.
:::

::
```
::

## Belgilar va izohlar

`badge` qisqa holat yoki toifani ko‘rsatadi. `note`, `tip`, `warning` va `caution` esa turli darajadagi izohlarni ajratadi. Ularning matnida nima uchun ogohlantirish berilayotganini tushuntiring.

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0"
---
  :::badge
  Tahrirlanmoqda
  :::

#code
```mdc
::badge
Tahrirlanmoqda
::
```
::

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0 [&>div]:*:w-full"
---
  :::note
  Bu sana ayrim manbalarda turlicha beriladi.
  :::

#code
```mdc
::note
Bu sana ayrim manbalarda turlicha beriladi.
::
```
::

::tip
Maslahat yoki qo‘shimcha ma’lumot uchun `tip`, ehtiyotkorlik talab qiladigan vaziyat uchun `warning`, jiddiy xavf uchun `caution` ishlatiladi.
::

## Karta va kartalar guruhi

`card` bir manbaga yoki tegishli sahifaga qisqa havola beradi. Bir nechta aloqador havola uchun `card-group` dan foydalaning.

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0 [&>div]:*:w-full"
---
  :::card
  ---
  title: Markdown sintaksisi
  icon: i-lucide-book-open
  to: /uz/docs/essentials/markdown-syntax
  ---
  Sarlavha, ro‘yxat va havola yozish qoidalari.
  :::

#code
```mdc
::card
---
title: Markdown sintaksisi
icon: i-lucide-book-open
to: /uz/docs/essentials/markdown-syntax
---
Sarlavha, ro‘yxat va havola yozish qoidalari.
::
```
::

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0 [&>div]:*:w-full"
---
  :::card-group{.w-full}
    ::::card
    ---
    title: Markdown
    to: /uz/docs/essentials/markdown-syntax
    ---
    Matnni tuzish.
    ::::
    ::::card
    ---
    title: Kod bloklari
    to: /uz/docs/essentials/code-blocks
    ---
    Kod namunasini ko‘rsatish.
    ::::
  :::

#code
```mdc
::card-group

::card{title="Markdown" to="/uz/docs/essentials/markdown-syntax"}
Matnni tuzish.
::

::card{title="Kod bloklari" to="/uz/docs/essentials/code-blocks"}
Kod namunasini ko‘rsatish.
::

::
```
::

## Yorliqli bo‘limlar

`tabs` bir mazmunning ikki ko‘rinishini, masalan, matn va kodni yonma-yon joylashtirish uchun mos keladi. Yorliqlarni sahifa tilida yozing.

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:my-0"
---
  :::tabs{.w-full}
    ::::tabs-item{label="Matn" icon="i-lucide-eye"}
    **Qalin yozuv**
    ::::
    ::::tabs-item{label="Kod" icon="i-lucide-code"}
    ```mdc
    **Qalin yozuv**
    ```
    ::::
  :::

#code
````mdc
::tabs

:::tabs-item{label="Matn" icon="i-lucide-eye"}
**Qalin yozuv**
:::

:::tabs-item{label="Kod" icon="i-lucide-code"}
```mdc
**Qalin yozuv**
```
:::

::
````

## Bosqichlar

`steps` ichidagi sarlavhalar ketma-ket raqamlanadi. `level` qiymati sarlavha darajasiga mos keladi.

::code-preview
---
class: "[&>div]:*:w-full"
---
  :::steps{level="4"}
  #### Manbani tanlang

  Ishonchli manbani toping.

  #### Matnni yozing

  Ma’lumotni o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan bayon qiling va havola qo‘shing.
  :::

#code
````mdc
::steps{level="4"}

#### Manbani tanlang
Ishonchli manbani toping.

#### Matnni yozing
Ma’lumotni o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan bayon qiling va havola qo‘shing.

::
````
