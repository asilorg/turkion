The **Ottoman–Persian Wars** also called the **Ottoman–Iranian Wars** were a series of wars between the [Ottoman Empire](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire> "Ottoman Empire") and the [Safavid](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safavid_Iran> "Safavid Iran"), [Afsharid](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afsharid_Iran> "Afsharid Iran"), [Zand](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zand_Iran> "Zand Iran"), and [Qajar](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_Iran> "Qajar Iran") states of [Iran](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran> "Iran") ([Persia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persis> "Persis")) through the 16th–19th centuries. The Ottomans consolidated their control of what is today [Turkey](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey> "Turkey") in the 15th century, and gradually came into conflict with the emerging neighboring Iranian state, led by [Ismail I](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_I> "Ismail I") of the [Safavid dynasty](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safavid_dynasty> "Safavid dynasty"). The two states were arch rivals, and were also divided by religious grounds, the [Ottomans](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire> "Ottoman Empire") being staunchly [Sunni](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni> "Sunni") and the [Safavids](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safavid_Iran> "Safavid Iran") being [Shia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia> "Shia"). A series of military conflicts ensued for centuries during which the two empires competed for control over eastern [Anatolia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia> "Anatolia"), the [Caucasus](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus> "Caucasus"), and [Ottoman Iraq](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Iraq> "Ottoman Iraq").

Among the numerous treaties, the [Treaty of Zuhab](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Zuhab> "Treaty of Zuhab") of 1639 is usually considered as the most significant, as it fixed present [Turkey](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey> "Turkey")–[Iran](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran> "Iran") and [Iraq](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq> "Iraq")–[Iran](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran> "Iran") borders. In later treaties, there were frequent references to the Treaty of Zuhab.
