Karakalpak is a Turkic language associated with Karakalpakstan and communities elsewhere in Central Asia. Glottolog identifies it as kara1467 (ISO 639-3: kaa) and supplies a bibliography including grammars and dictionaries. A language record and a political territory describe different things.

UNESCO’s joint yurt-making record includes Karakalpak craftspeople alongside Kyrgyz and Kazakh communities. Wooden construction, felt and textile work provide a concrete cultural subject beyond lists of states. Shared techniques do not erase local terminology and family histories. This short profile is a starting point for sourced coverage of literature, crafts and the changing environment around the Aral Sea.
